接駁 eARC Soundbar 後，PS5 和 Xbox 可以同時用 4K120 及 VRR 嗎？

可以，前提是 Soundbar 佔用 eARC 口後，電視仍有兩個同時支援 4K120 和 VRR 的輸入。

例子 如果 HDMI 3、4 都支援兩項功能，而 HDMI 3 是唯一 eARC 口，Soundbar 接 HDMI 3，PS5 接 HDMI 4。Xbox 就沒有另一個同時支援兩項功能的直駁端口。

適用限制 請按完整電視型號核對各端口規格和設定。此規劃器按裝置直接接電視計算。

Can PS5 and Xbox both use 4K120 and VRR with an eARC soundbar?

Yes, if the TV still has two inputs supporting both 4K120 and VRR after the soundbar takes the eARC port.

Worked example If HDMI 3 and 4 support both features and HDMI 3 is the only eARC port, connect the soundbar to HDMI 3 and the PS5 to HDMI 4. The Xbox then has no suitable direct input for both features.

Limits Check the exact TV model, per-port specifications and settings. This planner assumes direct connections to the TV.
